# Uber-Power-Bi-Dashboard
This project is a 5-page interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze Uber’s operational performance across revenue, vehicles, riders, locations, and customer behavior. It converts raw booking data into insights that support strategic decision-making on fleet allocation, pricing, customer retention, and service optimization.

---

## 📂 **Project Overview**

This project answers **how Uber is performing and why** by breaking down key metrics:

- Which **vehicle types generate the most revenue?**
- Where are **demand hotspots** across locations?
- What **payment methods** are most preferred by customers?
- How many rides are **completed, canceled, or incomplete**?
- What trends exist in **monthly / quarterly performance**?
- Where are customer & driver issues causing **avoidable losses**?

The dashboard is designed with a **UI-first approach** for clarity, business usability, and a real product feel.

## 📊 **Dashboard Pages**

| Page Name | Purpose |
|-----------|----------|
| **Home** | Welcome page, navigation UI, menu system |
| **Overview** | High-level KPIs & monthly trends |
| **Vehicle Insights** | Category-wise performance & revenue share |
| **Revenue Insights** | Payment method breakdown & revenue sources |
| **Riders & Drivers** | Customer stats, issues, complaints & losses |

---

## ✨ **Key Insights Delivered**

✔️ Auto, Go Mini & Go Sedan are the **top revenue drivers**  
✔️ UPI is the **most preferred payment method**, signifying digital-first riders  
✔️ Customer issues & over-capacity complaints lead to **avoidable lost bookings**  
✔️ Seasonal patterns show opportunities for **surge pricing strategies**  
✔️ Hotspot locations reveal where to **increase vehicle supply**
